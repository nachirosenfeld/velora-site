Questions, answered.

How fast can I get funded?

Most applications receive a decision within 24 hours. Once you accept an offer, funds often reach your account the same day or the next business day, depending on the funding partner and your bank.

Will applying affect my credit score?

Checking your options uses a soft credit pull, which does not affect your score. If you move forward with a specific offer, the funding partner may run a hard pull at that point, and your advisor will tell you before that happens.

What does it cost to apply?

Nothing. There is no application fee and no obligation to accept an offer. Velora Capital is compensated by the funding partner when a deal closes.

What do I need to apply?

Basic business details and recent business bank statements are enough to start. Depending on the product and the amount, your advisor may ask for tax returns or financial statements later in the process.

Do you lend the money yourselves?

No. Velora Capital is a business financing brokerage. We match your business with funding partners, we do not make the credit decision, and approval is never guaranteed.
