The questions owners actually ask.

How fast can I get funded?

Most applications receive a decision within 24 hours. Once you accept an offer, funds often reach your account the same day or the next business day, depending on your bank.

Will applying affect my credit score?

Checking your options uses a soft credit pull, which does not affect your score. If you move forward with a specific offer, a hard pull may be run at that point, and your advisor will tell you before that happens.

What does it cost to apply?

Nothing. There is no application fee, and no obligation to accept an offer once you see it.

What do I need to apply?

Basic business details and recent business bank statements are enough to start. Depending on the product and the amount, your advisor may ask for tax returns or financial statements later in the process.

How does Velora fund my business?

Two ways. Some deals we fund directly with our own capital. For others we draw on the hundred-plus funding sources we work with, so your business is matched to the structure that fits it. Approval and terms depend on your business profile and are not guaranteed.
